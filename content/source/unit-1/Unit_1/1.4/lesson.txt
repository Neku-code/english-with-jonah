LEARN WITH JONAH
UNIT 1 — LESSON 1.4
Yes/No Questions

1. SAVOL VA JAVOB

[RASM: images/01_questions_and_answers.png]

Rasmdagi suhbatlarni o‘qing.

A. Ben: Are you a student?
Emma: Yes, I am.

B. Maya: Are you tired?
Alex: Yes, I am.

C. Ben: Is Anna a student?
Emma: No, she isn't. She's a teacher.

D. Maya: Is it an app?
Ben: No, it isn't. It's a phone.

Yes — ha. No — yo‘q.

Savolda am/is/are oldinga chiqadi.

You are a student. → Are you a student?
She is ready. → Is she ready?
They are at home. → Are they at home?
I am late. → Am I late?

1. A rasmda Emma o‘zi haqida javob beryapti: Yes, ___ am.
Variantlar: you / I / she

2. B rasmga qarab, Alex haqida qisqa javob yozing: Is Alex tired?

3. C rasmga qarab, Anna haqida qisqa javob yozing: Is Anna a student?

4. D rasmda nima haqida so‘ralyapti?
Variantlar: Ben / Anna / telefon

2. SAVOL TUZING

Am I ...?
Is he/she/it ...?
Are you/we/they ...?

Is Anna a teacher?
Are Emma and Ben classmates?

Quyidagi gaplarni savolga aylantiring.

5. You are busy.

6. Emma is from Chicago.

7. The phone is on the desk.

8. Maya and Ben are in the library.

3. QISQA JAVOBLAR

Are you ready? — Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
Is he busy? — Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.
Is she a teacher? — Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
Is it a phone? — Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.
Are they classmates? — Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.

Ikki kishiga savol:
Are you students? — Yes, we are.

Siz ham kirgan guruh haqida:
Are we late? — No, we aren't.

Alex sizdan o‘zi haqida so‘rayapti:
Am I late? — No, you aren't.

Tasdiq qisqa javobida am/is/are qisqartirilmaydi:
Yes, I am. Yes, she is. Yes, we are.
Yes, I'm. yoki Yes, she's. deb javob berilmaydi.

Inkor javobda ikkala shakl ham to‘g‘ri:
No, she is not. = No, she isn't. = No, she's not.

Ma’lumotga qarab qisqa javob yozing.

9. Is it an app? Ma’lumot: Bu telefon.

10. Is Anna a teacher? Ma’lumot: Anna — o‘qituvchi.

11. Are you classmates? Siz va Emmadan so‘rashdi. Sizlar guruhdoshsiz.

12. Am I late? Alex sizdan so‘radi. U kechikmagan.

4. SUHBAT

Suhbatni o‘qing.

Maya: Are you at home?
Emma: No, I'm not. I'm at university.
Maya: Are you in the library?
Emma: Yes, I am.
Maya: Is Anna in the library?
Emma: No, she isn't. She's in the classroom.
Maya: Are you busy?
Emma: No, I'm not.

Suhbatga qarab qisqa javob yozing.
