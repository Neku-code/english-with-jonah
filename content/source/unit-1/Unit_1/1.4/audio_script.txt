AUDIO — 1.4

Bitta yozuv. Maya va Ben ovozlari. Ismlar ovoz chiqarib o‘qilmaydi.
Matn savollardan oldin ko‘rsatilmaydi.

Maya: Are you in the library?
Ben: No, I'm not. I'm in the classroom.
Maya: Is Anna at university?
Ben: Yes, she is.
Maya: Is she tired?
Ben: No, she isn't. She's busy.
Maya: Is the cafe open?
Ben: Yes, it is.

Ravshan, tabiiy sur’atda o‘qing. Replikalar orasida qisqa pauza. Fon musiqasi yo‘q.
