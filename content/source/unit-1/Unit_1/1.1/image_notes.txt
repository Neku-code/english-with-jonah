Joy: 1-bo‘lim boshida.
Vazifa: I bilan o‘zini tanishtirish; student va teacher farqi; it bilan telefon.
Tavsif: Amerika universitetida Emma va Ben o‘zini talaba deb, Anna o‘qituvchi deb tanishtiryapti. Stol ustida telefon bor. Har bir gap o‘z egasining nutq pufagida.
Matn: Hi. I'm Emma. I'm a student. / I'm Ben. I'm a student. / I'm Anna. I'm a teacher. / It's a phone.

Joy: 3-bo‘lim boshida.
Vazifa: I/you va we/they gapiruvchiga qarab o‘zgarishini ko‘rsatish.
A: Emma o‘zini ko‘rsatadi: I'm a student.
B: Emma Benga gapiradi: You're a student.
C: Emma o‘zi va Benni nazarda tutadi: We're classmates.
D: Maya o‘zi kirmagan Emma va Ben guruhini ko‘rsatadi: They're students.
Odamlarni almashtirib yubormang: we/they faqat odamlar soniga qarab tanlanmaydi.
