LEARN WITH JONAH
UNIT 1 — LESSON 1.1
Introduction “To Be”

1. TANISHUV

[RASM: images/01_meet_the_students.png]

Rasmga qarang va o‘qing.

Emma: Hi. I'm Emma. I'm a student.
Ben: I'm Ben. I'm a student.
Anna: I'm Anna. I'm a teacher.

I'm Emma. — Men Emmaman.
I'm a student. — Men talabaman.
I'm a teacher. — Men o‘qituvchiman.

I — men. You — sen/siz.
I am = I'm. You are = You're.

I am a student. — Men talabaman.
You are a student. — Siz talabasisiz.

Inglizchada am/is/are tushib qolmaydi: I am a student.

1. Rasmga mos gapni tanlang.
A) Emma is a teacher.
B) Emma is a student.
C) Emma is Ben.

2. Ben o‘zini tanishtiryapti. Gapni tugating: ___ am Ben.
Variantlar: You / I / He

3. Emma Benga murojaat qilyapti: ___ are a student.
Variantlar: I / He / You

4. So‘zlardan gap tuzing: student / am / a / I

2. ODAMLAR VA BUYUMLAR

He — u (erkak). She — u (ayol). It — u/bu (narsa).
He, she, it bilan is ishlatiladi.

Ben is a student. → He is a student.
Anna is a teacher. → She is a teacher.
It is a phone. — Bu telefon.

Birikmalarni birga o‘rganing:
a student — talaba
a teacher — o‘qituvchi
a phone — telefon
an app — ilova

A/an boshidagi tovushga qarab tanlanadi: a phone, an app.

5. Ben haqida: ___ is a student.
Variantlar: She / He / It

6. Anna haqida: ___ is a teacher.
Variantlar: It / He / She

7. Rasmda telefon: ___ is a phone.
Variantlar: It / She / He

8. Gapni tugating: Anna ___ a teacher.
Variantlar: are / is / am

3. BIZ VA ULAR

[RASM: images/02_speakers_and_groups.png]

A: Emma — I'm a student.
B: Emma Benga — You're a student.
C: Emma o‘zi va Ben haqida — We're classmates.
D: Maya Emma va Ben haqida — They're students.

We — biz. They — ular. You — siz/sizlar.
We, you, they bilan are ishlatiladi.

We are students. — Biz talabamiz.
They are classmates. — Ular guruhdosh.
You are students. — Sizlar talabasiz.

a student → students
a teacher → teachers
a phone → phones
an app → apps
a classmate → classmates

Ko‘plikda a/an ishlatilmaydi: We are students.
Emma and Ben are students. And — va.

9. C rasmda Emma gapiryapti: ___ are classmates.
Variantlar: They / We

10. D rasmda Maya Emma va Ben haqida gapiryapti: ___ are students.
Variantlar: We / They

11. Ikki talabaga murojaat: You ___ students.
Variantlar: is / am / are

12. Mos shaklni tanlang: We are ___.
Variantlar: a student / students

4. TO‘LIQ VA QISQA SHAKLLAR

I am = I'm
You are = You're
He is = He's
She is = She's
It is = It's
We are = We're
They are = They're

To‘liq va qisqa shakllarning ma’nosi bir xil.

Matnni o‘qing.

Hi. I'm Ben. I'm a student. Emma is a student. We're classmates. Anna is a teacher.

13. O‘zini kim tanishtiryapti?
Variantlar: Anna / Emma / Ben

14. We're classmates gapida we kimlarni bildiradi?
Variantlar: Ben va Anna / Ben va Emma / Emma va Anna

15. Qisqa shaklda yozing: They are students.

16. Gapni to‘g‘rilang: He is a students.
