LEARN WITH JONAH
UNIT 1 — LESSON 1.6
Final test

Taxminiy vaqt: 15–20 daqiqa.
20 ta topshiriq. Jami 24 ball.
1–16: har biri 1 ball. 17–20: har biri 2 ball.

Mustaqil bajaring. Javoblar alohida faylda.
Audio ikki marta tinglanishi mumkin. Tinglashdan oldin 13–16-savollarni o‘qing.

1. RASMLARGA QARANG

[RASM: images/01_exam_scenes.png]

1. A rasmga qarab tugating: Emma ___ in the library.
A) are
B) is
C) am

2. B rasmga mos gapni tanlang.
A) Alex is at home.
B) Alex is in the library.
C) Alex is in the classroom.

3. C rasmga qarab javobni tanlang: Is the cafe open?
A) No, it isn't.
B) Yes, it is.
C) No, they aren't.

4. D rasmga qarab javobni tanlang: What are they?
A) It's a phone.
B) They're phones.
C) They're students.

2. GAPLAR VA SAVOLLAR

5. I ___ tired.
Variantlar: is / are / am

6. Gapni inkor shaklga o‘tkazing: We are ready.

7. Gapni savolga aylantiring: She is from Boston.

8. Qisqa javobni to‘g‘rilang: Is Anna busy? — Yes, she's.

3. O‘QISH

Matnni o‘qing va 9–12-savollarga javob bering.

Hi. I'm Maya. I'm from New York. I'm at university. I'm in the classroom. Ben is from Boston. He's in the library. Anna is a teacher. She's busy, but she's fine.

9. Where is Maya from?
Variantlar: New York / Boston / Chicago

10. Where is Maya?
Variantlar: at home / in the classroom / in the library

11. Who is Anna?
A) She's a student.
B) She's Maya.
C) She's a teacher.

12. How is Anna? Matnda aytilgan ikkala holatni tanlang.
A) tired and late
B) busy and fine
C) ready and tired

5. YOZISH

Har bir topshiriqda bitta gap yoki savol yozing. To‘liq va to‘g‘ri qisqa shakllar qabul qilinadi.

17. Inglizchaga tarjima qiling: Men charchamaganman.

18. Ma’lumot: Telefon stol ustida. The phone bilan boshlanadigan gap yozing.

19. Sizga shunday javob berildi: I'm from Boston. You bilan qayerdan ekanini so‘raydigan savol yozing.

20. Siz va guruhdoshingizdan so‘rashdi: Are you ready? Ikkalangiz ham tayyorsiz. We bilan qisqa tasdiq javob yozing.
