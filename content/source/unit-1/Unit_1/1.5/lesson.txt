LEARN WITH JONAH
UNIT 1 — LESSON 1.5
Complex questions

1. MA’LUMOT SO‘RASH

[RASM: images/01_information_questions.png]

Rasmlarga qarang va o‘qing.

A. Where are you from? — I'm from Boston.
B. Where are you? — I'm in the library.
C. What is it? — It's a phone.
D. How are you? — I'm tired.

Where — qayerda. Where ... from? — qayerdan?
What — nima. How — qanday. Who — kim.

Bu savollarga kerakli ma’lumot bilan javob beriladi.
Is Ben at home? — No, he isn't.
Where is Ben? — He's in the library.

Mos savol so‘zini yozing.

1. ___ are you from? — I'm from Boston.

2. ___ is Ben? — He's in the library.

3. ___ is it? — It's a phone.

4. ___ is Alex? — He's tired.

2. WHERE / WHO / WHAT

Where is Emma? — She's at home.
Where are they? — They're at university.
Where is Ben from? — He's from Boston.

Who is Anna? — She's a teacher.
Who is he? — He's Ben.
Who are they? — They're Emma and Ben.

What is it? — It's an app.
What are they? — They're phones.

Savol tartibi:
Where + am/is/are + kim/nima?
Who/What + am/is/are + olmosh?

Where ... from? savolida from oxirida keladi.

5. So‘zlarni tartiblang: Emma / from / is / Where

6. Javobga mos savol yozing: They are in the classroom.
They va Where dan foydalaning.

7. Javobga mos savol yozing: She's Anna.
She va Who dan foydalaning.

8. Javobga mos savol yozing: It's a phone.
It va What dan foydalaning.

3. ISM VA HOLAT

Name — ism. My — mening. Your — sening/sizning.
Fine — yaxshi (holat).

What is your name? — Ismingiz nima?
My name is Emma. — Mening ismim Emma.

How are you? — Qalaysiz?
I'm fine. — Yaxshiman.
I'm tired, but I'm ready. — Charchaganman, lekin tayyorman.

Qisqa shakllar:
What is = What's
Who is = Who's
Where is = Where's
How is = How's

9. Ism so‘rash uchun mos so‘zni tanlang: ___ is your name?
Variantlar: Where / What / How

10. O‘z ismingiz bilan gapni tugating: My name is ___.

11. Mos javobni tanlang: How are you?
A) I'm from Boston.
B) I'm fine.
C) My name is Ben.

12. To‘liq shaklda yozing: Who's she?

4. EMMADAN XABAR

Matnni o‘qing.

Hi. My name is Emma. I'm from Chicago. I'm at university. I'm in the library. Anna is a teacher. She's busy. Ben is in the classroom. He's tired, but he's ready.

Matnga qarab javob yozing.

13. Where is Emma from?

14. Where is Emma?

15. Who is Anna?

16. How is Ben? Ikkala holatni ayting.

ESLAB QOLING
