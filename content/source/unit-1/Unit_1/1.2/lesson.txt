Locations and states

1. UNIVERSITETDA

[RASM: images/01_places.png]

Rasmlarga qarang va o‘qing.

A. Maya: I'm in the library.
B. Ben: I'm in the classroom.
C. Emma: I'm at home.
D. The phone: It's on the desk.

I'm in the library. — Men kutubxonadaman.
I'm in the classroom. — Men auditoriyadaman.
I'm at home. — Men uydaman.
It's on the desk. — U stol ustida.

Am/is/are dan keyin joyni aytish mumkin.

in the library — kutubxonada
in the classroom — auditoriyada
in the cafe — qahvaxonada
on the desk — stol ustida
at home — uyda
at university — universitetda

Bu birikmalarni shu shaklda eslab qoling.
The aniq joy yoki buyumni bildiradi: the library, the desk, the phone.
At home va at university birikmalarida the yo‘q.

Namuna: Maya → She is in the library.

1. B rasmga qarab, Ben haqida He bilan gap yozing.

2. C rasmga qarab, Emma haqida She bilan gap yozing.

3. D rasmga mos variantni tanlang: The phone is ___ the desk.
Variantlar: in / on / at

4. Biz universitetdamiz. So‘zlardan gap tuzing: university / We / at / are

2. QAYERDAN VA QAYERDA

Ben: I'm from Boston. I'm in Chicago.
Ben: Men Bostondanman. Men Chikagodaman.

From — -dan. In — bu gapda -da.

I'm from Boston. — Qayerdan ekanlik.
I'm in Boston. — Joylashuv.

5. Men Bostondanman: I'm ___ Boston.
Variantlar: in / from / on

6. Inglizchaga tarjima qiling: Emma Chikagodan.

7. Men Chikagodaman: I'm ___ Chicago.
Variantlar: at / from / in

8. Maya — Nyu-Yorkdan, hozir Bostonda. Mos gaplarni tanlang.
A) She's from Boston. She's in New York.
B) She's from New York. She's in Boston.
C) She's at home. She's from Boston.

3. HOLAT

[RASM: images/02_states_and_conditions.png]

Yuqori chap: Alex — I'm tired. — Men charchaganman.
Yuqori o‘ng: Anna — I'm busy. — Men bandman.
Pastki chap: The cafe — It's open. — U ochiq.
Pastki o‘ng: The cafe — It's closed. — U yopiq.

Yana ikki holat:
We are ready. — Biz tayyormiz.
You are late. — Siz kechikdingiz.

Holat bilan a/an ishlatilmaydi:
I'm a student. Ammo: I'm tired.

Sifatga ko‘plik -s qo‘shilmaydi:
He is busy. They are busy.

9. Alex haqida gapni tugating: He is ___.
Variantlar: tired / open / closed

10. Anna haqida gapni tugating: She ___ busy.
Variantlar: are / am / is

11. Pastki chap rasmga qarab gap yozing: The cafe ...

12. Pastki o‘ng rasmga qarab gap yozing: The cafe ...
