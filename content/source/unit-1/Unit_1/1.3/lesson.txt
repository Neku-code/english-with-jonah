LEARN WITH JONAH
UNIT 1 — LESSON 1.3
Negative statements

1. FAKTLARNI TEKSHIRING

[RASM: images/01_negative_statements.png]

Rasmlarga qarang va o‘qing.

A. Alex: I'm not at home. I'm in the library.
B. Anna: I'm not a student. I'm a teacher.
C. The cafe: It isn't open. It's closed.
D. Emma and Ben: We aren't teachers. We're students.

I'm not at home. — Men uyda emasman.
She isn't a student. — U talaba emas.
They aren't teachers. — Ular o‘qituvchi emas.

Inkor gapda am/is/are dan keyin not qo‘yiladi.

I am not tired.
He/she/it is not ready.
You/we/they are not at home.

Namuna: Alex is at home. → Alex isn't at home. He's in the library.

Rasmdagi ma’lumotga qarab yozing.

1. B rasm: Anna is a student. Gapni to‘g‘rilang. Anna isn't ... bilan boshlang.

2. C rasm: The cafe is open. Gapni to‘g‘rilang.

3. D rasm: Emma and Ben are teachers. Gapni to‘g‘rilang.

4. A rasmga mos ikkita gapni tanlang.
A) Alex isn't at home.
B) Alex is in the library.
C) Alex isn't in the library.

2. QISQA SHAKLLAR

I am not = I'm not
He is not = He isn't / He's not
She is not = She isn't / She's not
It is not = It isn't / It's not
You are not = You aren't / You're not
We are not = We aren't / We're not
They are not = They aren't / They're not

I bilan I'm not ishlatiladi. I amn't shakli ishlatilmaydi.

5. Men tayyor emasman: I ___ ready.
Variantlar: isn't / 'm not / aren't

6. Ben is not at home. Gapni isn't bilan qisqartiring.

7. We ___ not late.
Variantlar: is / am / are

8. Qisqa shaklda yozing: They are not classmates.

9. She's not busy bilan bir xil ma’nodagi gapni tanlang.
A) She isn't busy.
B) She is busy.
C) She's a student.

10. To‘liq shaklda yozing: We're not from Boston.

11. Gapni to‘g‘rilang: I amn't tired.

12. Gapni to‘g‘rilang: He not is a teacher.

3. ALEXDAN XABAR

Matnni o‘qing.

Hi. I'm Alex. I'm not from Boston. I'm from Chicago. I'm not at home. I'm in the library. Emma and I are classmates. We're ready. The cafe isn't open. It's closed.

13. Alex qayerdan?
Variantlar: Boston / Chicago / New York

14. Alex haqida qaysi gap matnga mos?
A) He's at home.
B) He isn't in the library.
C) He isn't at home.

15. Qahvaxona haqida qaysi gap matnga mos?
A) It's open.
B) It isn't open.
C) It isn't closed.

16. We're ready gapidagi we kimlarni bildiradi?
Variantlar: Alex va Emma / Alex va Anna / Emma va Ben

But — lekin.
I'm tired, but I'm ready. — Men charchaganman, lekin tayyorman.

5. MUSTAQIL MASHQ

Quyidagi gaplarni inkor shaklga o‘tkazing.

21. You are a teacher.

22. It is an app.

23. O‘zingiz haqingizda uchta gap yozing: bitta tasdiq va ikkita inkor gap. Joy, holat yoki talaba ekaningiz haqida yozing.

24. Gapni to‘g‘rilang: They aren't a tired.

23 dagi gaplaringizni ovoz chiqarib ayting.

ESLAB QOLING

I am not. He/she/it is not. You/we/they are not.
Not qo‘shilganda qolgan birikma saqlanadi: not a student, not ready, not at home.
