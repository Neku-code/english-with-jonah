## Curriculum

Unit 2 contains five teaching lessons and one final exam:

| Lesson | Focus | Practices | Vocabulary |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 2.1 | Daily routines; I/you/we/they | 12 | 12 |
| 2.2 | He/she/it; -s/-es/-ies; have/has | 12 | 12 |
| 2.3 | Don't/doesn't and base verbs | 12 | 12 |
| 2.4 | Do/does questions and short answers | 12 | 12 |
| 2.5 | Wh-questions and frequency | 12 | 12 |
| 2.6 | Final exam | 20 | Review only |

Original Uzbek explanations, English dialogues, substitution, gap filling,
sentence ordering and short reading checks follow the pedagogical structure of
the user's Side by Side PDF (printed pages 80–82, 88 and 100 consulted). No book
passages or artwork are reproduced. Five optional browser-TTS dialogues support
practice; the Unit 2 final exam has no audio dependency. A passing exam needs
18/20. Existing 30/50/10 rewards apply independently to each unit: 450 possible
coins per unit. Unit 2 does not require finishing Unit 1. Within each unit all
five teaching lessons must finish before the final exam opens.

Authoring: `python3 scripts/author-unit2.py`. Display, grading and audio are
separate; keys stay server-side. `pnpm unit2:publish` appends immutable content
version `2026-10-21.1`, validates task/vocabulary keys, and verifies an identical
second publication without overwriting history. No schema change is needed.
Production was inspected and already had all 11 migrations through 0010.

## Performance evidence and changes

Production inspection found Vercel functions in `iad1` (Washington) while Neon
is in `aws-ap-southeast-1` (Singapore). The adapter now targets `sin1` to colocate
server and database. This removes cross-region database round trips; actual
phone timings depend on the user's connection and were not measured here.

Lesson loading previously waited for home data, selected an attempt, then read
its content and reloaded that attempt's ownership. The new loader runs catalog
and owned revision lookup concurrently, then loads the answer snapshot. All
session, publication and requested-attempt ownership checks remain.

Final submission now stores all question marks in one statement instead of one
write per question, preserving answer revisions, automatic grading, transactions
and once-only rewards. Unit 2's exam reduces 20 answer writes to one.

No authentication cache, public student cache, sleep or fake instant grading is
introduced. Database cold starts and browser TTS availability still depend on
infrastructure/device support.
